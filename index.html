About me

I am a computational scientist in applied mathematics and physics, currently a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Basel (Department of Mathematics and Computer Science).

For five years I have been developing simulation code for engineering and physics problems — wave propagation, solid–fluid interaction, geophysics, electromagnetics and planetary thermal modelling — in C++, Fortran and Python, from PDE solver design and mathematical validation through to large-scale parallel runs (MPI/OpenMP) on HPC clusters.

Research interests

  • Numerical methods:

    •        -    Finite element methods (FEM)
           -    High-order nonconforming polytopal methods (HHO - HDG - dG)
           -    Unfitted methods (embedded / immersed)
           -    Explicit time integration and local time-stepping
           -    Nonlinear PDEs (variable-exponent problems)
           -    High-performance computing (MPI, OpenMP, OpenACC)

  • Application domains:

    •        -    Wave propagation (elastic, acoustic, seismic)
           -    Geophysics
           -    Solid and fluid mechanics
           -    Electromagnetics
